# LensFlow Creator Recruitment — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Velvet Broadcast
Very Brief Intro: A cinematic creator studio aesthetic built from deep plum, black glass, warm skin tones, and soft magenta light. It makes signing up feel like stepping onto a premium live stage.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Creator Commons
Very Brief Intro: A bright editorial community direction with generous paper-like space, candid photography, and a warm coral accent. It emphasizes belonging and approachable creator economics.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Signal After Dark
Very Brief Intro: A restrained dark-interface direction with electric pink signals, live-status markers, and a broadcast-control-room mood. It highlights immediacy and creator autonomy without becoming cyberpunk.
Probability: 0.09

## Selected approach: Velvet Broadcast

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial nightlife meets premium broadcast identity: cinematic portraiture, gallery-like spacing, and polished studio hardware used as visual evidence of a real live experience.

### Core Principles
The page should feel intimate without being explicit, premium without being cold, and decisive without resorting to hype. Visual hierarchy should move from human presence to the revenue split to the signup action. Every section should have a strong focal point, generous breathing room, and one clear action.

### Color Philosophy
The palette uses ink black and deep aubergine as a private-room foundation, soft ivory for readable editorial type, and LensFlow magenta as the ownable signal of live presence. A champagne highlight is reserved for the 81% figure so the economics feel valuable and legible rather than loud.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetric split-screen opening: the supplied creator image occupies the visual weight while a dark editorial panel carries the promise and signup action. Lower sections alternate wide text rails with compact proof cards, avoiding a repetitive centered marketing grid.

### Signature Elements
A slim magenta live-status line will recur as a broadcast motif. Oversized percentage typography will act as an economic anchor. Fine hairline rules and small uppercase labels will create a research-desk-like sense of clarity inside the cinematic system.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like tuning into a live channel: buttons brighten and lift slightly, status dots breathe subtly, and content reveals remain quick and quiet. CTAs should never feel like traps; every action should clearly communicate whether it opens signup, the creator dashboard, or a placeholder flow.

### Animation
Entrance motion uses opacity and short upward transforms under 260ms, staggered by 50ms. The hero image receives a slow, barely perceptible scale drift only when reduced motion is not requested. The live-status dot uses a restrained pulse. Buttons use a 150ms press scale and a sharp ease-out hover response.

### Typography System
Use DM Sans for interface copy and Archivo Black for high-impact display numerals, with Cormorant Garamond for a few editorial pull lines. Headlines should use tight tracking and compact line-height. Supporting copy should stay between 15–18px with generous line-height. Avoid all-caps paragraphs; reserve uppercase for labels and concise CTAs.

### Brand Essence
LensFlow is the private live-show platform for adult creators who want a more direct, personal way to connect with an audience and keep a clear share of show revenue. Personality: intimate, confident, transparent.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and human. CTAs are active but never desperate. Microcopy explains the next step in plain language and keeps the revenue split unambiguous.

Example lines: “Your room. Your audience. Your share.” and “Go live when you’re ready.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact L-shaped broadcast aperture mark paired with the LensFlow wordmark. The mark should feel like a camera iris opening into a live channel, not a generic play icon.

### Signature Brand Color
LensFlow Signal Magenta: #F15AA8. Use it as a focused live-status accent, CTA highlight, and editorial underline rather than as a full-page gradient.

## Style Decisions

The supplied creator image is the primary hero asset and must remain visually recognizable. The website promotes adult creators but will use tasteful, non-explicit presentation and an 18+ qualifier. The confirmed offer is “Creators receive 81% of show revenue”; do not claim creators earn 81% more than another platform or imply guaranteed income.
